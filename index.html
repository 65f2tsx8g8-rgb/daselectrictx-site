About DAS Electric

DAS Electric is a family-owned electrical contractor serving homeowners, businesses, and builders in Austin and surrounding areas. With more than 20 years of hands-on experience, we deliver thoughtful, code-compliant work for custom homes, pools, service upgrades, generators, EV chargers, and small commercial projects.

You can expect clear communication, dependable scheduling, and craftsmanship that holds up. We take pride in doing the job right, respecting your property, and leaving every project safer, more capable, and ready for what's next.
